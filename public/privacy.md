# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Overview

Image Compressor is committed to protecting your privacy. Our service is designed to compress images directly in your browser without ever uploading your data to our servers.

## Data Collection

**What we DON'T collect:**
- Your images
- Personal information
- Browsing history
- Device identifiers
- Location data

**What we DO collect (optional):**
- Anonymous usage analytics via Plausible Analytics
  - Page views
  - Referrer information
  - Approximate location (country-level only)
  - Device type and browser

## How It Works

All image compression happens **locally in your browser** using standard web APIs. Images are processed in-memory and never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

## Cookies

We do not use cookies. Analytics are cookie-free and privacy-respecting.

## Third-Party Services

- **Hosting:** Vercel (CDN and server infrastructure)
- **Analytics:** Plausible Analytics (privacy-focused, no cookies)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Know what data is collected about you
- Request deletion of any personal data
- Opt out of analytics collection

For privacy concerns, email: privacy@imagecompressor.cloud
